Our History

Traveling as if we were inside a memory.

Two friends passionate about travel, nature, and the magic of Studio Ghibli films studied Tourism Management with the dream of creating experiences different from traditional agencies.


Inspired by worlds like Princess Mononoke and Spirited Away, they decided to combine tourism with the artistic, emotional, and contemplative sensibility that these stories convey.


Thus Orumi Journeys was born: a space where every journey seeks to feel like an intimate, warm and transformative adventure.

Our Essence
